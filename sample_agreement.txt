RESIDENTIAL LEAVE AND LICENSE AGREEMENT (SAMPLE - FICTIONAL)

This Agreement is made at New Delhi on 1st November 2026 between Mr. Rakesh Malhotra (the "Licensor"/Landlord) and Ms. Ananya Sharma (the "Licensee"/Tenant) for the premises situated at Flat 204, Sunrise Apartments, Saket, New Delhi.

1. TERM: The license is granted for a period of 11 months commencing 1st November 2026.

2. RENT: The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 22,000 on or before the 5th day of each month. Delay beyond the 5th will attract a penalty of Rs. 500 per day.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT: The Tenant has paid an interest-free security deposit of Rs. 1,10,000 (equal to 5 months' rent). The deposit shall be refunded within 90 days after the Tenant vacates the premises, after deductions the Landlord considers necessary.

4. LOCK-IN PERIOD: The Tenant shall not vacate the premises before completion of 12 months. In case of early exit, the Tenant shall forfeit the entire security deposit and pay an additional 3 months' rent as penalty.

5. RENT ESCALATION: The rent shall be increased by 15% on renewal, and the Landlord may increase the rent at any time during the term if property taxes or market rates rise.

6. MAINTENANCE: The Tenant shall pay society maintenance charges as may be decided by the Landlord from time to time, along with electricity, water and any other charges.

7. REPAIRS: The Tenant shall bear the cost of all repairs including structural repairs, seepage and damage to fittings, whether or not caused by the Tenant.

8. ACCESS: The Landlord or his representatives may enter the premises at any time without prior notice for inspection.

9. TERMINATION: The Landlord may terminate this Agreement by giving 7 days' notice without assigning any reason. The Tenant shall give 3 months' written notice to terminate.

10. SUBLETTING AND GUESTS: The Tenant shall not allow any guest to stay overnight without prior written permission of the Landlord.

11. JURISDICTION: Any dispute shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts at New Delhi.
